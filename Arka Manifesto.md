# Manifesto [draft 1]

## Why Arka exists?
Arka surges as a movement with the intention to recover the really heart of internet:

-**Decentralized structure**

-**Personal websites without algorithms**

-**Organic discoveries**

-**Identity separated for your real life**

-**Deep and meaningful conversations**


## The values it stands for?

-**Free speech.**

-**Decentralization is our strength.**

-**Privacy by default.**

-**Meaningful content.**
